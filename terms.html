1. What ReelForge is

ReelForge ("the app") is a tool run by Kyriacos Sakkas ("I"), Nicosia, Cyprus. It makes short videos and uploads them to accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok. It runs on my own server. This website describes the app; it does not collect any data itself.

2. Who may use it

Today only I use the app, and only for accounts that I own. There is no public sign-up. If the app opens to more users, these terms will be updated first, and every user will only be able to connect accounts they own or are allowed to manage. If you somehow reach the app without being given access, you may not use it.

3. Connecting an account

When an account is connected (for example TikTok through TikTok's Login Kit), the account owner agrees that the app may use the permissions they grant for what is described in the Privacy Policy. On TikTok that means: reading the basic profile (name, picture, @username), and uploading videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts, which the owner then posts in the TikTok app. If Direct Post is turned on later, it also means posting videos to that TikTok profile with the privacy and interaction settings the owner chooses. An account can be disconnected at any time, in the app or in the platform's own settings.

4. The content

Every video is reviewed and approved before it is uploaded. The account owner is responsible for it and for following each platform's own rules (for TikTok: the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines).

5. No warranty

The app is provided as is. It may stop working, for example when a platform changes its API.
