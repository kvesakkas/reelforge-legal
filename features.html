Features

Everything ReelForge does between an idea and a published short video.

Series and scripts

Series, not single videos

A series has a niche (scary history, mythology, festive stories, facts…), a tone, a language and a voice. ReelForge keeps producing new episodes for it.

Scripts that fit short video

Each script has a strong first line, a clear story and a short ending, sized for 30 to 60 seconds. Topics a series already covered are not repeated.

English and Greek

Scripts, voices and captions in both languages, including the Greek alphabet in captions.

Script preview

Optionally, each script waits for you to approve, rewrite or edit it before voice and images are made.

Formats

Story episodes, single-idea videos and other formats per series.

Duplicate a series

Start a new series from the settings of one that works.

Video production

AI voice-over

Natural neural voices with adjustable speed, with a fallback voice if a provider is unavailable.

One image per scene

Images generated in the series' art style, with slow camera motion and depth so the video never feels static.

Word-by-word captions

Large, readable captions timed to the voice, with highlighted key words.

Music

Optional background music mixed under the voice.

Vertical MP4

Rendered as a 1080×1920 video ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, also downloadable.

Retries

A failed step (voice, image, render) can be retried or regenerated from the dashboard.

Review and publishing

Review first

Every video waits in Review. Approve it, reject it or regenerate it. Auto-approve can be turned on per series.

Schedule

Set posting times per series. Approved videos are published at the next free slot, with a daily limit per account.

Five platforms

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Pages, Threads and TikTok, through each platform's official API.

TikTok inbox drafts

Videos are sent to the creator's TikTok inbox. The creator gets a notification in the TikTok app, edits if they want and posts it.

Per-platform text

Titles, captions and hashtags are written for each platform; a caption can be copied with one click.

Status for every post

See where each video was posted, where it is waiting and why something failed.

Accounts and security

Official logins

Accounts are connected with each platform's own login and consent screen (OAuth). ReelForge never sees your passwords.

Encrypted tokens

Access tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored, and deleted at once when you disconnect an account.
