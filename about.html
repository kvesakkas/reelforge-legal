About ReelForge

Who makes it, and where it is going.

Who runs it

ReelForge is built and run by Kyriacos Sakkas, an independent developer in Nicosia, Cyprus.

Why it exists

Kyriacos runs faceless short-video channels: scary history, Greek mythology, festive stories and more, in English and Greek. Making every video by hand (writing, recording, finding images, captioning, uploading to five apps) took far longer than the video itself. ReelForge started as his own tool to do those steps as one pipeline, while he stays in charge of what is published.

Where it is today

ReelForge is in active use for his own channels: it produces videos every day and publishes them to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok. It runs on a private server in the cloud.

It is now being prepared for more users: other creators who want the same pipeline for their own channels. Until that is ready there is no public sign-up; if you are interested, get in touch.

Principles

  • A person approves what is published. Review comes before posting, and on TikTok the creator always posts the draft themselves.
  • Only official APIs. Every platform is used through its official API and login, within its rules.
  • Minimal data. Only what is needed to post is stored, encrypted, and deleted on disconnect. See the Privacy Policy.
