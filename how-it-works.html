How it works

Four steps from an idea to a published video.

1

Create a series

In the dashboard, a short wizard asks what the series is about (for example scary history or Greek mythology), its tone, language, voice and speaking speed, the art style of the images, background music, and how many videos a day to make and at what times to post. You also choose which connected accounts it posts to.

2

ReelForge makes the episodes

For each episode ReelForge:

  • writes a script for a topic the series has not covered yet,
  • records the voice-over,
  • creates one image per scene in the chosen style,
  • times captions to every word,
  • renders a vertical 1080×1920 MP4 with music.

If you turn on script preview, it stops after the script so you can edit it first.

3

You review

Finished videos appear in Review. Watch each one and approve, reject or regenerate it. Approved videos move to “Ready to post”, rejected ones never leave ReelForge.

4

It gets published

At the series' posting times, ReelForge uploads each approved video through the platform's official API, with a title, caption and hashtags written for that platform:

  • YouTube – uploaded as a Short to the connected channel.
  • Instagram – published as a Reel on the connected professional account.
  • Facebook – published as a Reel on the connected Page.
  • Threads – published as a video post.
  • TikTok – sent to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. TikTok notifies the creator, who opens it in the TikTok app, adds sounds or edits if they like, and posts it. ReelForge never posts on TikTok by itself.

The dashboard shows, for every video, where it was posted, where it is still waiting and the link to each post.

Connecting an account

In Settings → Connections you press “Connect” next to a platform. You are taken to that platform's own login and consent screen, where you see exactly which permissions ReelForge asks for. After you agree, you are sent back to ReelForge and the account shows as connected. ReelForge never sees your password. “Disconnect” deletes the stored tokens at once; you can also remove access in the platform's own settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
